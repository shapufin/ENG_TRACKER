HOW CALENDAR SHARING WORKS

Share calendar visibility across teams

When you assign the same calendar group to multiple teams, they can see each other's leave, overtime, and standby entries in the Calendar page.

Example Assign group 'Regional-Pod-A' to MSC_TEAM and SIAE_TEAM => Both teams see each other's calendar entries in the workspace selector.

Sharing workflow

Quick setup guide

1

Select teams from the table below

2

Type a group name (e.g., msc-siae-shared)

3

Click 'Apply to selected'

4
